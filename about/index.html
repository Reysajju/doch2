About Us

At Baluchi Doch, we are passionate about preserving and elevating the traditional art of Baluchi dress making. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide exceptional customization services that honor the rich heritage of Baluchi culture while meeting the contemporary needs of our clients.

Our Story

Founded by a family of master tailors, Baluchi Doch brings generations of expertise to every piece we customize. Our workshop in Punjab has become a center of excellence where traditional craftsmanship meets modern design sensibilities.

Our Commitment

We are committed to delivering the highest quality of workmanship while maintaining the authenticity of Baluchi dress traditions. Each piece that passes through our workshop receives meticulous attention to detail and is handled with the utmost care.
